Who is he

Melbourne to the grid

Oscar Jack Piastri was born on 6 April 2001 in Melbourne — a stone's throw from the Albert Park circuit that hosts the Australian Grand Prix. He didn't start in go-karts. He started in remote-control car racing, winning a national title at just nine years old before switching to karts at ten.

Then came one of the cleanest runs junior motorsport has ever seen: three championships in three straight years — the 2019 Formula Renault Eurocup, the 2020 FIA Formula 3 title, and the 2021 FIA Formula 2 title (which he won by more than 50 points).

He made his Formula 1 debut with McLaren in 2023, taking Rookie of the Year. His first Grand Prix win came at the 2024 Hungarian Grand Prix, and he was a key part of McLaren winning back-to-back Constructors' Championships in 2024 and 2025. In 2025 he racked up seven wins and 16 podiums — both records for an Australian driver.
